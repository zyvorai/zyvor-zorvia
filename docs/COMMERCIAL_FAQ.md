# Commercial FAQ

Prices referenced here are the launch prices in [PRICING.md](PRICING.md).

**Do I need a license?**
Yes. Production Use requires a commercial license from Zyvor AI Labs Private Limited (sales@zyvor.dev); a 30-day trial key is available for production evaluation. See [LICENSE](../LICENSE) and [NOTICE](../NOTICE).

**What do I pay for, then?**
The commercial license for Production Use, and services around the software: production support, deployment, migration, training and managed operations. Support is a paid service contract.

**Will Zorvia stop working if my contract lapses?**
No. Expiry only ends the paid service. It never stops VMs, disables VM APIs or existing functionality, or blocks access to your data.

**Is there a license key or feature gate?**
No. Contracts are records of what you bought. The product does not enforce them.

**What is a billable node?**
A Kubernetes worker node covered by your contract. The order form lists covered clusters and nodes and states how mixed-role nodes, temporary nodes and capacity changes are treated.

**How is annual support calculated?**
The greater of (worker nodes × per-node price) and the annual minimum.

**Do I pay for Managed and Supported?**
No. Managed pricing includes the contracted support for the same coverage. Do not add a second support subscription for it.

**Does Managed give you access to my cluster?**
No. Buying a contract grants no access. Remote operation, if you want it, is a separate opt-in with scoped, revocable credentials.

**Do you offer 24×7 support or resolution deadlines?**
24×7 response applies to Severity 1 and 2 on Supported Plus and, by default, Managed. Supported is business hours. Hours, timezone, holidays and response targets are written in your contract ([SUPPORT_SCOPE.md](SUPPORT_SCOPE.md)). Targets are for first response. Resolution times are estimates, not commitments.

**Are experimental features covered?**
Not unless your contract says so in writing. See [FEATURE_MATURITY.md](FEATURE_MATURITY.md).

**What is not included in the prices?**
Hardware, hosting, third-party subscriptions, travel and applicable taxes, plus anything "separately scoped" in [SUPPORT_SCOPE.md](SUPPORT_SCOPE.md).

**Are international prices converted from India prices?**
Supported and Supported Plus: yes, the USD price is the India price excluding 18% GST (INR / 1.18 / 84, rounded). Managed and Migration are set in USD, and the India figure is USD x 99.12 including GST. In short: INR including 18% GST in India; US dollars excluding tax elsewhere (VAT, GST or sales tax added where applicable).

**How do I pay?**
Online by UPI, card or netbanking where a buy page exists at [zyvor.dev/buy](https://zyvor.dev/buy); international cards work (charged in rupees); or request a US-dollar invoice by bank transfer. Custom pricing: sales@zyvor.dev.

**How do I get a quote?**
[Book a demo](https://zyvor.dev/schedule?utm_source=github&utm_medium=zorvia&utm_campaign=docs_commercial_faq) or write to sales@zyvor.dev with your cluster and worker-node counts.
